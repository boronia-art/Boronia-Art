Our classes

Pick a batch by age — not by skill

Every child starts where they are. We build skill through play, projects and plenty of paint.

Little Doodles

Ages 4–6
  • Scribble-to-shape drawing games
  • Finger painting & crayon play
  • Colour, pattern & story art

Focus: fine motor skills, colour sense and the joy of making a happy mess.

Most loved

Young Sketchers

Ages 7–10
  • Pencil drawing & observation
  • Watercolour & oil pastels
  • Folk-art & festival projects

Focus: real technique — shading, composition, patience — still wrapped in fun.

Creative Masters

Ages 11–14
  • Perspective, shading & charcoal
  • Acrylic painting on canvas
  • Portfolio & exam-board guidance

Focus: personal style and a portfolio — for exams, competitions, or the love of it.

About the studio

A studio that feels like a friend's living room

Boronia Arts classes are deliberately small, so every child gets attention and every idea gets heard. We never grade art against the child next door — we compare each artist only with the artist they were last month.

  • A no-pressure classroom — effort is celebrated, not just results
  • A new theme every month, from monsoon skies to festival art
  • Every artwork goes home, framed or celebrated — never binned

Good questions

Everything parents ask us

What ages do you teach?

Children from 4 to 14 years, grouped into three batches — Little Doodles (4–6), Young Sketchers (7–10) and Creative Masters (11–14). Every child gets age-right projects, from scribble games to canvas painting.

Do we need to bring any materials?

No — Yes or you can purchase from us : paints, brushes, paper, canvas, everything.

Can we try a class before joining?

Yes! Every child gets one free trial class — no payment, no forms to sign. Use the form below or message us on WhatsApp to reserve a seat.

What are the fees?

✏️ ₹ — 1000/- per month, per child, materials included. Fees are collected monthly; we'll share the details when you book your trial class.

Where exactly are you located?

✏️ B404,Bhoomi Kshitij Society,Rahatani,Pune-411017 — see the map pin in the contact section below.

What if my child misses a class?

Life happens! We'll help your child catch up in the next session
